How the System Operates

A transparent look at the core pipeline from resume parsing to shortlisting.

1
Resume Upload

Candidate uploads their resume file. The parser extracts text automatically from PDF or DOCX.

2
ATS Score Breakdown

The system calculates layout sub-scores and provides personalized suggestions to improve keywords.

3
Semantic Job Match

Candidates apply to posted positions. An NLP-based Cosine Similarity algorithm scores resumes against job descriptions.

4
Rank & Shortlist

Recruiters examine matched applicant lists, filter by keyword attributes, and shortlist candidates.
