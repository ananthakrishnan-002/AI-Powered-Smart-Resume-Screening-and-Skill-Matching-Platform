\vspace{1cm}


The AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform is an intelligent web-based application designed to streamline and automate candidate shortlisting, resume parsing, and applicant ranking. The system enables the human resources (HR) personnel to evaluate candidate resumes based on specific job descriptions, required skills, experience, and educational background. The recruiters can upload batches of candidate resumes in various formats like PDF or DOCX. The AI-based approach offers a parsing feature for extracting key entities and matching candidates to the job requirements, which is easier and more efficient than manual reading. The system can be used in varying recruitment needs, including hiring for IT roles, management positions, technical support, administration, and general corporate roles.
The application also incorporates an automated ranking system that can calculate a compatibility score for each candidate and offer digital dashboards for applicant tracking. The administrative portal can be used for effortless job posting management, while the HR module is effective for managing candidate data, scheduling interviews, and collecting recruitment reports. Overall, the application is beneficial by minimizing the manual work, eliminating human bias, enhancing hiring speed, and providing excellent talent acquisition services. In addition, the digital nature of the system reduces the use of paper, thus saving costs and supporting environmental sustainability.
The project is associated with several of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). It contributes to SDG 4 – Quality Education by matching candidates' educational backgrounds and skills to appropriate opportunities, promoting lifelong learning and skill development, and providing insights into the industry's educational requirements. Finally, the project will also contribute to achieving SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth because it will result in increased productivity while reducing human bias and other related negative aspects of traditional manual screening. It will also help HR departments to embrace digitalization in their process.
In conclusion, the AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform project will be a great and smart way of managing recruitment because it is efficient, cost-effective, fair, and meets the SDGs.

\section{Project Overview}
AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform is a web based application which is used to facilitate the task of analyzing candidate resumes, extracting their qualifications, and managing the associated recruitment activities. Companies are nowadays receiving hundreds of applications for various roles including programming, office administration, management, graphic design, and content creation. As the requirement for a job role varies from one position to another, therefore, the required skill set is also different for every opening. The technical roles typically require high-end programming skills, framework experience, and technical degrees while the administrative roles need strong communication, data processing, and office management skills. Similarly, the recruiters and HR professionals have specific requirements according to the job descriptions they handle. Therefore, it becomes difficult to manually read and shortlist the right candidates that fit the specific requirement of a company without spending excessive time. The proposed system presents an AI powered smart platform where a recruiter will be able to parse and rank the desired candidates based on their skills and job requirements.

The system will provide the facilities to select the required job postings, upload multiple resumes, extract contact information, and match the candidate's skills to the requirements. The artificial intelligence-based recommendation will enable the HR professional to choose the desired candidates without having to manually read every single document line by line. Instead of comparing different physical documents, the user will enter the job description and upload the resumes, and they will get recommended candidate rankings that can be reviewed according to their hiring needs. It will ease the process of candidate shortlisting while reducing human bias. The proposed system is therefore suitable for the different categories of recruiters including corporate HRs, startup founders, technical interviewers, and general talent acquisition teams.

One more feature of the proposed system that might be considered valuable is the automated candidate ranking process, which would calculate the compatibility score of the candidates. Following the upload of the resumes, the user will see the extracted candidates with corresponding percentage scores before finalizing the interview shortlist. The system will also assist the HR department to save time and effort by reducing or eliminating the possibility of human oversight and managing the related applicant tracking statuses. The candidate personal information, extracted skills, job details, and related ranking information can be managed with the help of the MySQL database. The administrative interface of the application will be helpful in managing the job postings, candidate scores, interview stages, and recruiter details.

The project is related to providing equitable opportunities where the application will decrease the dependence on biased human decisions. In the same way, the recommendation system will help the recruiters to make smart, unbiased decisions for the selection of potential employees. The SDGs addressed by the project are SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth and SDG 4 – Quality Education. The application is designed by using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the front-end and Python, PHP for back-end development while using MySQL for database management and Apache Server through XAMPP. Therefore, the AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform will provide a smart digital platform for the recruitment process with the recommendation system and automated ranking with database management.

\section{Objectives}

The main aim of the AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform is to design an application that will help HR professionals to create a smart and easy-to-use screening workflow. Candidate resumes can be difficult to evaluate as there are numerous differences between each document's layout, formatting, terminology, and structure. Recruiters handling large volumes may not have the time to thoroughly read every single document to see if they are the best fit for their intended role. The proposed system aims to address the problem using an artificial intelligence (AI) extraction tool which will present recruiters with candidate summaries fitting their intended job description and requirements. The system intends to address not only to parse text documents but also make the process accessible and easy to understand for regular HR staff.

Another objective is to provide a customized job matching facility through which the users can score candidates against specific criteria. Each job's skill needs differ, and thus the resume screening system should enable the users to modify the job descriptions as needed. For example, a recruiter looking for a Python developer may need a candidate with Django experience, while another user hiring for a frontend role may strictly need React. The system should aim to provide a personalized candidate ranking experience as opposed to providing the recruiters with rigid keyword matching results.

The system also intends to automate text extraction and scoring processes. Manually calculating a candidate's fit for the various job requirements can be cumbersome, and biased mistakes can easily be made. The proposed system will automatically compute the compatibility percentage for the selected candidates using Natural Language Processing (NLP) against the job descriptions in its database. Besides that, it will also generate digital candidate profiles to ensure that the recruitment process is quick and efficient. The idea is to make screening intuitive and avoid biases that may be made when reviewing resumes manually. The system will also store information about the candidates and their scores in a database to help keep track of electronic records of the applicants.

In addition, the project aims to enhance the overall administrative and applicant tracking functions of the HR department. The HR administrator should be able to update the information regarding the job postings, required skills, candidate statuses, and interview schedules using the application. By digitally storing the information, it will save time and effort from manually recording and updating candidate pipelines. Hence the project aims to improve the overall efficiency of the HR department management while at the same time enhancing recruitment accuracy.

Finally, the project is expected to promote equal opportunities based on merit. By focusing on candidates' real skills and educational qualifications rather than subjective factors, the system ensures a fair evaluation process. Moreover, the ranking system will ensure that recruiters only interview the best candidates. The project will promote sustainable development since it will utilize technology, encourage unbiased hiring, and automate the screening process, thus advancing SDGs 8 and 4. In summary, the project will provide an effective, intuitive, and efficient solution for resume screening and applicant tracking.

\sectionsection{Scope of the Project}
The scope of AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform includes the key activities involved in creating job postings, uploading candidate resumes, parsing document text, generating compatibility scores, and managing relevant applicant information. The system is designed as a web based application, which can be accessed through a suitable web browser. The recruiter can register and log into the application and then provide a job description for the required position. Based on the specified requirements and skills, the system can provide AI based recommendations for suitable candidates. The user can review these recommendations and change the candidate status according to the interview stages.

The scope of the project consists of managing major recruitment entities like job postings, candidate profiles, extracted skills, contact information, compatibility scores, and interview statuses present in the system. The candidate details, extracted data, and resume files can be stored in the MySQL database. The recruiter can view the ranked candidates and select their preferred applicants during their hiring process. The system can calculate the total compatibility score of each candidate to know how well they match the final job description. The dashboard facility prepares digital candidate profiles for the chosen applicants.

In addition, the project involves administrative processes. An administrator can control job information, make changes to the requirements, maintain candidate pipeline information, view recruiter data, and manage overall hiring. Consolidated data management facilitates the update process and minimizes the maintenance effort. The administrator can use the system to ensure the accuracy of applicant information presented to hiring managers. It will help improve the overall organization of the corporate recruitment process.

The current implementation of the project is carried out using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the frontend, Python and PHP for the backend, MySQL database, and Apache Server via XAMPP. The scope of the project currently encompasses the limited number of features available in these technologies. More sophisticated machine-learning mechanisms like deep learning language models, synchronization with major job boards, advanced automated email scheduling engines, cloud-based version, and mobile application are the potential future improvements that could be considered for further development and enhancement of the present solution. Thus, the current project provides an opportunity to create a prototype of the intelligent resume screening and tracking system, leaving the opportunity to improve and expand the scope within the capabilities of the chosen programming tools.

\section{Introduction to SDGs}
The Sustainable Development Goals(SDGs) are a collection of global development goals established by the United Nations to address key economic, social, environmental, and technological issues. Sustainable development refers to the improvement of the general well-being and the increase in economic opportunities while maintaining the responsible use of resources and avoiding adverse effects on the future generations. In the current technological age, software applications and new technologies like Artificial Intelligence can contribute to achieving sustainable development by enhancing productivity, conserving resources, promoting innovation, and helping organizations be socially responsible.

The AI-Based Resume Screening System is related to sustainable development because it uses digital technology to enhance an existing human resources practice. The software uses artificial intelligence to recommend to recruiters which candidates to interview based on their skills and the job description, which helps eliminate biased hiring decisions. The system also eliminates the need for paper-based resume printing, thus promoting sustainability. In addition, automating the screening and tracking processes as well as providing information on applicants would make the overall HR operations more efficient and less tedious.

The project is especially relevant to sustainable development as it incorporates both technological progress and fair employment. Recruitment has various critical socio-economic impacts that can be optimized only if the recruiter can efficiently match skills to requirements. The application can help the user to avoid interviewing unqualified candidates or even missing out on highly qualified applicants due to human fatigue. Moreover, digitization of HR processes can also reduce the consumption of paper and optimize the work of many corporate departments.

Thus, the project shows how a technological solution can go beyond its business scope. For my project, I have selected SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth and SDG 4 – Quality Education as goals that can be impacted by my project. These goals relate to the main aspects of my system, including automation, Artificial Intelligence, digitalization, fair employment, and promoting skill-based learning opportunities.

\section{Selected SDG(s)}

The AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform relates to SDG 8 and SDG 4, considering the specificities of this project. SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth emphasizes the importance of sustainable economic growth, productive employment, and better working conditions. The new system contributes to this goal by automating a number of repetitive processes associated with resume reading. For example, manual parsing, applicant tracking, candidate ranking, and other record-keeping activities can be systematized and taken up with fewer efforts by the human resources employees.

On SDG 4 – Quality Education, the target is to ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all. The proposed project is highly connected to this goal as it matches applicants based on their educational background and acquired skills. By providing clear, objective analysis of what skills are currently demanded in the job market, it also helps candidates understand the educational requirements of various industries, thus promoting lifelong learning and targeted skill development.

The choice of these two SDGs can be explained by the project’s objectives. The application allows for increasing economic efficiency by implementing processes’ automation and adopting innovative technologies, such as Artificial Intelligence, while also selecting candidates based on more accurate data on their education and skills. Thus, the project can be regarded as an example of how a software product can contribute to sustainable development by addressing a relevant HR problem.

\section{Problem Addressed}
The AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform addresses a number of issues associated with conventional recruitment and applicant tracking practices. One of the major problems is the challenge experienced by recruiters in evaluating hundreds of resumes. Resumes are comprised of unstructured text and the relevance of the candidate depends on how their skills match the job description. Recruiters with limited time may have a hard time comparing the documents, understanding the skill overlaps, identifying suitable candidates, and shortlisting applicants within a strict deadline resulting in the possibility of biased hiring or loss of top talent.

Another issue that is associated with traditional hiring methods is the absence of an intelligent recommendation system, which is often needed in modern corporate environments. This problem is particularly acute for technical roles since there are numerous variations of technical skills and a manual review is unable to cope with diverse formats of different resumes. For example, a candidate may have the right skills but format their resume poorly, causing a human recruiter to overlook them. In addition, different roles will have different requirements as well. However, the proposed solution offers an NLP recommendation system that takes into account different job descriptions and extracts skills objectively.

Manual sorting is another important issue that the project will address. Reading a number of resumes manually can be an extra step that requires much time and leads to human bias. Keeping physical resumes and applicant files can impede retrieval of information. The proposed system will automate the extraction of the text and provide digital candidate scores that will reduce effort and improve accuracy in recruitment.

The project also solves applicant tracking and information-management issues. A conventional system stores candidate details, resumes, statuses, job descriptions and interview schedules in separate spreadsheets. These records may become out-of-date or hard to deal with as the number of applicants increases. The proposed system keeps relevant information in a central MySQL database that allows administrators to manage information efficiently. By these features, the project addresses the problems related to resume parsing, bias reduction, ranking accuracy, record management and operational efficiency.


\section{Contribution of the Proposed Project towards the SDG(s)}
The proposed AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform will create value according to SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth, as it is geared towards improving the efficiency of human resources activities. Conventional recruitment activities can lead to huge wastage of employee efforts due to repetitive activities like reading documents, extracting contact details, logging applicant statuses and updating interview records. Such activities can be automated through the proposed system thereby eliminating such monotonous activities and enabling productive activities such as candidate interviewing and strategic hiring. Increased work productivity and fair hiring can be a major contribution towards the SDG.

The project contributes towards attaining SDG 4 – Quality Education, as it uses the power of Artificial Intelligence to objectively evaluate the educational and skill-based merits of the candidates. The proposed AI based recommendation engine has potential to change the way resumes are being evaluated by the recruiters. Instead of relying upon the intuition of the HR person, the system emphasizes the actual educational qualifications and competencies of the candidate. This objective matching process encourages candidates to continually improve their education and skills to meet real-world job demands.

The synergies between these 2 SDGs demonstrate how the proposed project idea could be a viable solution to a modern-day HR problem while enabling economic development and promoting skill-based education.

\section{Expected Social and Educational Impact}
The proposed system will have a positive social impact by enabling fair, merit-based hiring for all applicants. The resume screening process can be prone to human bias requiring a recruiter to ignore demographic details such as names, gender, or age. By having an AI recommendation tool, the recruiters will be able to shortlist candidates that will suit their roles without the unconscious biases that affect human decision making. This promotes equal opportunity and decent work for all sections of society.

The system will have a positive economic impact by automating the parsing and record-keeping processes. It will decrease the amount of manual reading that the HR employees will have to deal with, enabling them to focus on interviewing the candidates instead of wasting their time and energy on menial sorting tasks. Same benefits will be bestowed upon the management, who will be able to utilize the system's information management capabilities to organize and view the hiring pipeline and candidate data.

The project will have a positive educational impact as well. By consistently highlighting the gap between a candidate's current skills and the job requirements, it encourages individuals to pursue further education and upskilling. Thus, the project will have a positive impact on the learning ecosystem by making industry demands more transparent.

In general, the project will have a positive impact on society, the economy, and the educational landscape, thus meeting the requirements of SDG 8 and 4. It will create value for recruiters by providing them the relevant recommendation of top candidates, save the HR's time and effort in paperwork, and reduce bias by focusing on actual educational merit.

\section{Existing System / Problem Statement}
The current system involved in resume screening and candidate shortlisting is a manual process between the HR recruiter and the submitted applicant documents. In this process, the recruiter communicates the needs and expectations of the job such as required skills, education, experience, and the candidate submits a resume that outlines their qualifications. Though manual, this process is reliant on the reading speed of the recruiter and the formatting of the candidate's resume. Individuals who are tasked with evaluating hundreds of resumes are unable to determine which candidates are the absolute best without spending hours reading. As such, they end up rejecting candidates that are highly qualified due to fatigue or poor resume formatting. The manual nature of the process also necessitates subjective decision making by the recruiter before shortlisting a specific candidate.

Another limitation of the current system is the absence of an intelligent and systematic NLP matching mechanism. Various job roles have different skill requirements and therefore a simple keyword search cannot satisfy every technical nuance. In a manual system, finding the best candidate requires the individual analysis of each resume and its context. Recruiters may also have difficulty determining whether the candidate's skills are semantically related to the job description (e.g., matching "Machine Learning" to "Artificial Intelligence"). This can lead to missed talent or biased shortlisting. The existing system generally lacks a centralized platform through which recruiters can enter job descriptions and get ranked candidate suggestions in an organized manner based on semantic similarity.

Like candidate tracking and record management, they are also done manually or through different disconnected systems. When multiple resumes are received for a job posting, the candidate data has to be extracted by manually typing the details into a spreadsheet. Not only is this process error-prone as data is entered or candidates update their profiles, but there is also a need for maintaining applicant statuses, interview schedules, job details, and hiring pipelines manually, which are hard to search anytime information is needed. The resumes may also require downloading, printing, and space for storage before they are archived in the system, which is not only time-consuming but requires extra effort in record maintenance. The limitations in the current system that lead to the need for an AI-Based Resume Screening System include dependence on manual reading, no semantic NLP intelligence, time-consuming data entry, human bias, and lack of efficient applicant management.

\section{Proposed System / Solution Overview}

The proposed AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform aims to resolve the limitations of the present manual screening and tracking process through the provision of a central online platform. The system enables HR users to enter job descriptions and upload batches of resumes and get appropriate AI-based recommendations on the best candidates. Instead of asking recruiters to manually read a range of documents, the application facilitates them through the parsing process and presents a more convenient method of ranking candidates. The user can go over the recommended applicant leaderboard and adjust the candidate status according to the interview stages and finalize the hiring pipeline.

The proposed system will maintain the information of job postings, candidate profiles, and necessary tracking data in a MySQL database. During the operation of the resume upload facility, the application pulls the relevant text from the documents, processes it with Python NLP, and displays the extracted information on the user interface. Upon processing the documents the system automatically calculates the compatibility score using TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity. This will give the recruiter an insight into how well the candidate matches the required job description. Once parsed, the system then processes the candidate profile and the digital dashboard is generated. Thus, the system eliminates the need to perform manual reading and enhances fairness in the screening process.

The proposed system also has administrative functions that allow it to manage information required by the application. Administrators can maintain job details, update required skills, manage candidate statuses, view applicant data and handle recruitment pipelines. Since the information is stored centrally, it can be managed more efficiently and the latest available candidate data can be presented to hiring managers. The system thus combines HR facing resume screening with basic administrative and talent acquisition functions.

The application was designed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the front end, Python and PHP to manage the back end, MySQL as the database management system, and Apache Server from XAMPP as the server. The proposed system in the study offers a modified version of the conventional manual method by incorporating artificial intelligence NLP, automated candidate ranking, digital tracking, and database management to ease the recruiter's experience while hiring their desired candidates. The proposed system aims to offer a modified approach to the manual method of evaluating a resume and managing the activities in a corporate HR department.

The major features of the proposed system are:

\begin{itemize}
\item AI-Based NLP Parsing for Resumes
\item Automated Candidate Ranking
\item Semantic Compatibility Scoring
\item Digital Candidate Dashboard
\item Applicant Tracking System
\item Job Posting Management
\item MySQL Database Management
\item Management of HR Admin
\item Development of User-Friendly Interface

\end{itemize}

\section{Module Description}
The proposed AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform can be seen as a collection of modules forming a system allowing for job management, resume parsing, candidate scoring, applicant tracking, and administration. These modules are connected to each other via the PHP web application and MySQL database. This setup enables HR users to create jobs, define their requirements, upload resumes, generate AI scores, and manage the candidate pipeline. Modules related to administration allow for the management of users, jobs, candidates, and overall data.

\textbf{Module 1: User Authentication and Authorization}

This module is responsible for providing controlled and secured access to the system. It requires HR professionals and administrators to register and log in to the application using their credentials. The module ensures confidentiality by comparing the hashed credentials entered by the user to those stored in the database. In addition, it creates a PHP session when a user logs in with the correct credentials. Moreover, this module enables the recruiter to view jobs, upload resumes, and manage candidates. This module also allows the administrator to access the system back-end. It also allows the administrator to add and delete jobs and track the activity of the HR staff. Lastly, this module terminates the session when the user logs out of the application.

\textbf{Module 2: Job Posting Management}

This module allows the HR user to create, read, update, and delete (CRUD) job postings. The user can specify the job title, a detailed text description, and the mandatory skills required for the role. These job postings serve as the anchor for the AI screening process, as the job description text is heavily utilized by the NLP engine to calculate candidate compatibility.

\textbf{Module 3: Resume Ingestion and Text Parsing}

This module handles the bulk uploading of candidate resumes. The web interface accepts PDF files and securely saves them to the server directory. Once uploaded, the Python backend extracts the raw text from the documents, stripping away complex formatting and images, preparing the clean text for semantic analysis.

\textbf{Module 4: AI NLP and Semantic Scoring}

The AI Engine is the “brain” of the app which scores the candidates. It utilizes Spacy for Named Entity Recognition to extract the candidate's Name, Email, Phone, and Skills. It then uses the Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) algorithm to vectorize both the resume text and the job description. Finally, it calculates the Cosine Similarity between the two vectors, outputting a percentage-based compatibility score that determines how well the candidate matches the job.

\textbf{Module 5: Candidate Ranking and Applicant Tracking}

This module will handle all the candidate data once the AI has finished processing the resumes. The system will store all the relevant extracted information and the generated score in the database. The module will display a ranked leaderboard of the candidates on the dashboard, sorted by their similarity score. The HR user can view detailed profiles, see the matched skills, and update the candidate's pipeline status (e.g., Pending, Shortlisted, Rejected). Having a digital dashboard for the candidates will make the tracking process easier and more convenient, making the system more eco-friendly.

\textbf{Module 6: Administrative and Data Management}

This module is solely available to the authorized administrators who can perform diverse operations related to the maintenance and control of the system. Firstly, the administrators can perform standard operations to manage HR user accounts. Secondly, the administrators can oversee the entire recruitment data, viewing all active job postings and the total candidates processed. Finally, with this module, the administrators can ensure that the MySQL database is functioning correctly and that no unauthorized access occurs. Altogether, this module allows administrators to operate the system by managing its users, jobs, candidates, and data integrity.



\section{Data Design}
The AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform will be using a relational database management system, specifically MySQL, to store, manage, and retrieve data and information within the system. This system will center around the information about the HR user accounts, job postings, candidate profiles, extracted skills, and compatibility scores, and make sure that only the relevant information is available to the users and that the information is related properly.

\textbf{Users Table:}
The Users table contains all the HR personnel of the system. Such users’ data as User ID, Full Name, Email, Password Hash, and Role are stored in the database. The Role column implies that one can be either a standard HR recruiter or an administrator. The latter can control and manage the former. Also, the user’s data is connected to the data of the user’s created job postings.

\textbf{Jobs Table:}
The Jobs table is a core part of the application because it stores all the recruitment openings, including their Job ID, User ID (creator), Job Title, Job Description, Required Skills, and Status (Active/Closed). This data is used to anchor the candidate uploads and provide the necessary text for the AI similarity comparison.

\textbf{Candidates Table:}
The database named “Candidates” contains information about the applicants after their resumes have been processed by the AI. It consists of Candidate ID, Job ID, Candidate Name, Email, Phone, Extracted Skills, Resume File Path, Similarity Score, and Pipeline Status (e.g., Shortlisted, Rejected). The table is beneficial since it allows the system to retain the parsed NLP data and provide HR users with an appropriate tool for managing and tracking the applicants based on their AI score.

\textbf{Database Relationships:}
The database tables were linked by using primary and foreign keys to maintain the integrity and consistency of data. In the proposed database schema, User ID links the HR users to their respective job postings in the Jobs table, and through the use of Job ID, the jobs are related to their respective applicants in the Candidates table. In this case, therefore, 1-to-N relationships have been made between the tables to minimize data redundancy. This will ensure that information is retrieved fast enough during the generation of the ranking leaderboards, parsing of resumes, and tracking of applicants.

\section{Implementation Overview}
Implementation is a stage in the process of developing an innovation idea, which involves introducing it into practice. In this regard, the development of the AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform involved the implementation of the designed system requirements. Initially, the development involved the installation of the local server using the local XAMPP application. Secondly, the project involved the design of the database using the MySQL phpMyAdmin application. The application’s backend system was developed using the PHP and Python programming languages. The project’s frontend system was designed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The implementation process was done in phases, starting with the development of the database and authentication system to protect the system and follow-up by developing the HR panel for managing the job postings. The project’s core NLP engine, which comprises the resume parser, AI recommendation algorithm, and candidate dashboard, was implemented in the final phase.

\section{Implementation Procedure}

\textbf{Development Environment Setup}
The development environment was prepared using Visual Studio Code, XAMPP
Server, PHP, MySQL, Python, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript. The XAMPP Server was used
to provide the local Apache web server and MySQL database environment. The Python environment was configured using pip to install NLP dependencies like Spacy and Scikit-Learn. The project files were organized into appropriate folders for the user interface, backend processing, database connectivity, and AI scripts.

\textbf{Database Implementation}

Using phpMyAdmin, the necessary MySQL database tables, including
$\texttt{users}$, $\texttt{jobs}$, and $\texttt{candidates}$, were created. The primary key and foreign
key relationships were established for the tables to maintain data
integrity and consistency. The $\texttt{users}$ table was populated
with HR accounts for development and testing purposes.

\textbf{User Authentication Implementation}

The user authentication procedure was implemented by utilizing PHP sessions and
MySQL database to store the relevant information. The HR professional is able to register
by entering the desired credentials. Then, the user has to log in by entering
the same credentials to gain access to the system. The user can perform specific
actions depending on the access level. The log out button was also added to the
dashboard in order to ensure security.

\textbf{Job Posting Management}

The job management section was implemented to provide access to the
recruitment openings. The HR user can input job titles, descriptions, and required skills. The job details
such as description text are saved to the database and used heavily by the AI engine as the anchor for comparison.

\textbf{Resume Parser Implementation}

The text extraction section was implemented using Python's PyPDF2.
When a user uploads a PDF, the PHP backend saves the file and uses `shell_exec` to call the Python script. The script reads the binary PDF, extracts the raw string text, and cleans it for the NLP pipeline. 

\textbf{AI Recommendation Implementation}

The recommendation functionality based on Artificial Intelligence was
implemented to help the HR users choose the candidates that suit their
jobs. The system uses Spacy for Named Entity Recognition to extract names and skills, and uses Scikit-Learn to apply the TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity math. The system analyzes the data, and scores the applicant. The given suggestions
may be reviewed by the user in order to update the candidate pipeline status.

\textbf{Dashboard and Ranking Implementation}

The ranking dashboard was created to store candidate profiles and their scores in order
to allow users to easily track the best applicants before the actual interview.
The candidates are displayed in a dynamic data table, sorted automatically by their compatibility percentage in descending order.

\textbf{Applicant Tracking}

The tracking module is designed to record interview decisions in the
database. After reviewing a profile, the recruiter can change the status to 'Shortlisted' or 'Rejected'. The system stores the recruiter's decision, updating the centralized candidate pipeline.

\textbf{Administrative Functions}

The administrative functions were implemented in the system to give the
authorized administrators control of the data in the system. These
administrators manage the overall jobs, view HR users, and monitor overall applicant volume. This ensures appropriate
information is maintained in the system securely.

\textbf{Testing and Final Deployment}

Once the major modules were implemented, the system was tested to evaluate the
user authentication process, the creation of jobs, uploading of resumes, extraction of text, checking of similarity, AI recommendations, ranking process, and status updates. Various test cases were
designed and executed to detect and eliminate errors. Finally, the system was
tested locally in the XAMPP environment to ensure that all NLP scripts and
database elements were working properly.
\subsection{User Training}
All users are guided through the basic steps in using the AI Based Resume
Screening System through the user-friendly web interface. The
guidance includes navigating the dashboard, creating a
detailed job description, and using the Upload Interface to submit
a batch of PDF resumes as well as understanding the AI compatibility
score generated by the system.

The training teaches how to review candidate profiles, check the
extracted skills, update the applicant status, and manage the interview pipeline.
Users learn how to make objective hiring decisions based on the semantic score after completing the upload phase.

Administrators are given basic instructions for using the
administrative functions of the system. They are told how to manage
HR user accounts, oversee job postings, view system analytics, and
monitor data integrity. They are also given basic instructions for handling common
Python execution errors and maintaining accurate system security.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{System Maintenance}

Timely maintenance ensures that the AI Based Resume Screening
System remains reliable and efficient. The Python NLP libraries (like Spacy and NLTK)
must be consistently updated to maintain accurate text extraction, Named Entity Recognition, and parsing performance. This supports the AI recommendation features in
providing accurate candidate scores to recruiters.

Periodic checks of the MySQL database are also essential to ensure the
integrity of user accounts, job text, candidate data, scores, and
tracking information. Database backups should be performed on a regular basis
to avoid data loss in the event of system failures. In addition, administrators
should also ensure that old, rejected resumes are deleted to free up disk space.

The PHP scripts, HTML, CSS, Python files, and JavaScript files must be periodically
reviewed to discover possible errors, security breaches, and performance
issues. It is also essential to regularly test the login, job creation,
resume upload, NLP execution, scoring, and status update features after
significant updates or modifications to ensure their continued
functionality.
The system must be checked for proper database connection and session
management, and the affected functions should be re-tested and
repaired if any errors are found. It is also necessary to perform regular
maintenance to ensure the server environment is compatible with the
software and that PHP, Python, MySQL, and relevant configurations run correctly.
Before making significant software or NLP model updates to the system,
careful consideration must be given to their potential impact on the
existing parsing accuracy.
\end{itemize}

\section{Implementation Results}
The deployment of the AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform
has enabled the development of a working prototype that allows HR recruiters to post jobs,
upload resumes, and rank candidates. The system comprises the frontend interface,

complete automated recruitment experience.

The user authentication function was implemented to enable HR users to
register and log on to the system securely. After logging in, the users can

The system automatically calculates the compatibility percentage of the parsed
candidates and saves them in the database. Users can

The administrative functionality provides authorized administrators with

The developed solution allows creating the process that includes the user's

provides an initial version of the system for the HR departments. However, there

\section{Limitations}

While the AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform successfully meets its
primary objectives as a functional prototype, certain limitations exist in
the current implementation:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Complex Document Formatting:} The current system requires standard text-based PDFs. The Python PyPDF2 library struggles with highly stylized resumes containing complex columns, images, or unusual fonts, leading to jumbled text extraction.

\item \textbf{Basic AI Algorithms:} The current scoring
functionality is based on TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity. The
system does not utilize sophisticated deep learning algorithms
like BERT to truly understand sentence context beyond semantic keyword vectors.

\item \textbf{Language Constraint:} The system currently only supports English resumes. The Spacy NLP models and stop-word dictionaries are optimized for English and cannot parse or score candidates who submit resumes in other languages.

\item \textbf{Local Development:} The system was developed and tested on a local XAMPP server. It is not designed or optimized to host on a large scale and synchronous Python execution might cause timeouts during massive bulk uploads.

\item \textbf{No OCR Capabilities:} The prototype version of
the system does not offer Optical Character Recognition to extract text from scanned images or JPEG resumes.
\end{itemize}


\section{Future Scope}

The created system can be the basis for future improvements that would
increase its intelligence, scalability, and applicability in the real world.
Some of these developments could include:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{More advanced Deep Learning models:} The current
TF-IDF algorithm can be replaced
with more advanced AI and machine learning models (like Transformer networks) that would analyze
the applicant's experience context, grammatical structure, and industry nuances much better.

\item \textbf{Asynchronous Processing:} It is possible to
enhance the system by connecting it to background task queues like Celery, so that hundreds of resumes can be uploaded at once without crashing the web browser, notifying the HR via email when done.

    \item \textbf{Email Automation Integration:} Secure SMTP APIs can be integrated to automatically email rejection letters to low-scoring candidates or interview invites to top-ranked applicants.

\item\textbf{OCR Engine:} A comprehensive OCR tool like Tesseract can be
integrated to allow the system to read scanned image PDFs, significantly broadening the supported file types.

\item \textbf{Cloud Deployment:} The application and database can be migrated to the cloud (AWS/Heroku) to
allow secure remote access, scale up computing power for the AI engine, and
improve overall system availability.

    \item \textbf{Candidate Portal:} A dedicated applicant-facing
portal can be developed to enable candidates to directly type their experience, apply to jobs, and track their own status, bypassing the need for PDF uploads entirely.

\item  \textbf{Enhanced HR Analytics:} Future versions can
offer detailed recruitment analytics, bias-auditing reports, and predictive hiring metrics to assist companies in making
more knowledgeable talent acquisition choices.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}

The AI-Powered Smart Resume Screening and Skill Matching Platform presents a practical and
integrated web-based solution for simplifying the process of resume parsing,
candidate evaluation, ranking, and applicant tracking. By combining an intuitive user
interface with AI-based NLP recommendations, manual job creation, automated
semantic scoring, text extraction, and digital dashboards, the system
reduces the complexity involved in modern recruitment. The
system also allows HR recruiters to evaluate candidates according to objective skills and
job requirements, making the screening process more fair and unbiased to applicants of all backgrounds.

The application of centralized administrative functions is an organized
way of managing jobs, HR users, candidate data, and interview pipelines.
The combination of PHP, Python, and MySQL is a right choice for effective
execution and storage of the system data, and digital profile generation
minimizes the dependence on paper-based physical files. The project proved the
practical application of web development, database management, Natural Language Processing,
and AI-based mathematical scoring in the human resources field.

Overall, the created prototype meets the main goals of the project, and there is
potential to build upon its development in future endeavors. It is possible
to integrate more advanced deep learning machine learning algorithms, provide asynchronous background processing, include thorough OCR scanning, implement email automation, deploy the application in a cloud-based
environment, and introduce a candidate-facing portal. Moreover, the project aligns with
the achievement of SDG 8 and SDG 4 by contributing to equitable
educational merit, optimizing corporate operations, and promoting fair, unbiased, and sustainable employment practices.
